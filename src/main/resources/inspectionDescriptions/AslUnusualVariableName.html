Reports a key in Assign, in a state of any query language, that is not a plain variable name, where it is not known whether Step Functions rejects it: a name with a character other than a letter, a digit or _ that is not ., - or a space, such as a combining mark. A plain variable name starts with a letter, holds only letters, digits and _, and has at most 80 characters.

The names Step Functions is known to reject are reported as errors by the inspection for invalid variable names.
