Reports a key in Assign in a nested state machine, a Parallel branch or the item processor of a Map, whose name a state machine around it assigns before the flow enters the Map or Parallel state. Step Functions rejects the definition: the name is already defined in a parent scope. This holds for a Distributed Map too, although its states cannot read the outer variable.

An outer assignment counts when the flow leads from it to the Map or Parallel state, also through a loop: from a state's own Assign along its Next and its Default only, not its Choice rules (a Choice state's own Assign applies only when no rule matches) nor its Catchers; from a Catcher's or a Choice rule's Assign along its own Next. The Map or Parallel state's own Assign and the Assign of its Catchers follow the same rule: they count when the flow leads from them back to the state, the state itself as its own Next included. Assignments that come only after the Map or Parallel state, with no way back to it, are not reported. Two Parallel branches that assign the same name are not reported either. Where the flow of the outer state machine is not known, for example through a placeholder Next, nothing is reported.
