Reports an object that lacks a field it must have: StartAt or States of a state machine, Type of a state, Resource of a Task, ErrorEquals of a retrier or catcher, Next of a catcher or of a top-level Choice rule, Condition of a JSONata Choice rule, a time field of a Wait state, and similar.

AWS documents that it rejects such a definition, so this is reported as an error. An object that holds a YAML merge key that could not be applied is not reported, because the merge may supply the field.
