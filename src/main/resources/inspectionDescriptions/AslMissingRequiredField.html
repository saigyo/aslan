Reports an object that lacks a field it must have: StartAt or States of a state machine, Type of a state, Resource of a Task, Parameters or Arguments of a Task that calls a service integration, RoleArn in Credentials, ErrorEquals of a retrier or catcher, Next of a catcher or of a top-level Choice rule, Condition of a JSONata Choice rule, a time field of a Wait state, and similar.

AWS rejects such a definition, so this is reported as an error. A service integration needs its parameters even when the call takes none; an empty mapping is enough then. An object that holds a placeholder key or a YAML merge key that could not be applied is not reported, because either may supply the field.
